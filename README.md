# FirstHour Malaysia

The first safe hour after a scam. For international students in Malaysia who have just realized they were scammed (fake police, immigration or courier calls): add your evidence, get a prioritized plan, a bank-call script, an evidence timeline, and a warning about "recovery fee" follow-up scams. English, Malay and Bangla.

Entry for Devpost's [Build With AI: Basics](https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/). Deadline: **Oct 26, 2026, 5:00 pm ET** (Oct 27, 5:00 am Malaysia time).
