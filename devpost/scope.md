# FirstHour Malaysia

The first safe hour after a scam: for someone who has just sent money to a scammer, a calm, sourced plan for what to do right now, in their own language.

> Drafted from decisions already made (idea, Malaysia focus, prize goal, "nobody has built it") and the market check. Items marked *(proposed)* are new and need approval.

## The Unique Kernel
It starts **after the loss**. Every scam entry found in this event works before the money moves: ScamShield AI and RakshaOS detect scams, Second Key is a call-back check before a parent pays, JobXray checks job offers. None helps once the transfer is done.
Two beats make it memorable *(proposed)*:
1. Every extracted fact is pinned to the exact line of evidence it came from, and every phone number or step links to a dated official source.
2. The follow-up message ("pay a fee and we will recover your money") is caught and flagged. The second scam is the one nobody warns victims about.

## Who It's For
An international student in Malaysia (the demo persona is Bangladeshi) who sent about RM 1,200 by bank transfer minutes ago after a fake police or immigration call. They are panicking, English is their second language, and today they search several official pages while the minutes pass.

## The Core Loop
They add what they have (pasted messages or a screenshot, payment method, time since the loss). FirstHour shows the facts it found with their evidence, a prioritized list ("call your bank, then 997") with tap-to-call links and sources, a filled call script to read out, and a timeline. Later messages can be pasted to check for recovery scams. *(proposed)* It is a one-time emergency tool, so there is no reason to "come back" beyond the next suspicious message.

## Inspiration & Identity
No references given yet. *(proposed)* Calm, clear and human, like a careful friend who knows the procedure; not an alarming "cyber" look. Works on a phone-sized screen.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Prize money is the goal: "Focus on Goal not the Holes." The condition is that nobody has built it. Lives in Malaysia as a student from Bangladesh, so the problem and the languages are close to home. Leads a team of four and a company (OQVIAN GROUP) that builds AI and security systems.

## What "Working" Looks Like *(proposed)*
The demo video opens on a fake "police" message and "RM 1,200 sent 4 minutes ago". Then:
- facts appear beside their evidence snippets;
- "Call your bank, then NSRC 997" comes first, with dated official sources;
- the user copies the filled call script;
- the timeline appears;
- a "we can recover your money for a fee" message is pasted and the warning shows (the "oh, that's cool" beat);
- a printable summary is shown;
- the app switches to Bangla once on camera, all at phone width.

## The POC Boundary *(proposed)*
- Bank transfer under one hour done well, plus e-wallet; about 6 to 8 sourced steps.
- English complete; Bangla for the emergency steps with a human review. No Malay in the app: nobody on the team can review it. The call script the user reads to the bank is in English.
- The recovery-fee scanner.
- Live AI extraction on fictional evidence, with fixture mode as a fallback. If the video uses fixture mode, the description says so.
- Numbers and steps come only from the sourced data file. The AI never produces them.

## Later
- Card, cash deposit and crypto paths, and the "over 24 hours" paths.
- Malay. Moves here because the team has no Malay reviewer; wrong emergency wording could cause harm. Add it when a reviewer is found.

## Explicitly Cut
- Contacting banks or filing reports for the user: the tool must never act on their behalf.
- Any promise of recovering money: it cannot be guaranteed and would be misleading.
- Accounts, login and storing evidence: privacy, and nothing to protect if nothing is kept.
- A chatbot or a general scam detector: detection is the crowded cluster in this event.
- Other countries: the official channels differ and cannot be sourced in the time.
- Live AI translation of emergency text: a wrong translation here could cause harm, so the wording is fixed and reviewed.
