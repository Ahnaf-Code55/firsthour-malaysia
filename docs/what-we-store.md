# What we store

FirstHour keeps your answers, evidence, screenshots, and extracted facts in your browser's memory while the page is open. It does not save them to a FirstHour account or database. If you use live reading, evidence is sent to the FirstHour extraction endpoint and then to the AI provider selected by the project. Provider processing follows that provider's terms. Do not use real victim information with Gemini's free tier; use fictional examples only. Closing or reloading the page clears the app's in-memory data.
